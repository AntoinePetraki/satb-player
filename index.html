Voice coach

Sing your part live into the microphone, or upload a recording. You see your pitch against the notes, get scores for right notes, intonation and rhythm, and what to work on.

How to record
  1. Pick your part, the bars and a tempo in the controls above. Slower is fine; the coach measures you at that tempo.
  2. For live singing, wear headphones so the microphone hears only you. Without headphones, turn your own part off in “Voices to hear”. Tap Sing live, allow the microphone, and sing along: your pitch shows in the strip above the play button, and your results appear when the passage ends or you tap pause.
  3. To use a recording instead, record yourself singing the same bars (only your voice on it) and tap Upload recording. The coach finds where you start on its own.
